# Rafael Camacho's personal site

I'm Rafael Camacho, PhD, Head of Unit – Operations at the [Centre for Cellular Imaging](https://www.gu.se/en/core-facilities/core-facilities-research-infrastructures/centre-for-cellular-imaging) and Head of Unit for [Integrated Microscopy Technologies Gothenburg](https://www.scilifelab.se/units/integrated-microscopy-technologies/) at SciLifeLab. I am also an SSF Research Infrastructure Fellow.

My work focuses on advanced light and electron microscopy, bioimage analysis, software development, and the interplay between image analysis and automated microscopy to create smart microscopy workflows. My academic background is in chemical physics; more information is available in my CV.

:::{admonition} SSF Research Infrastructure Fellowship
In 2022, I was selected as a Research Infrastructure Fellow by the [Swedish Foundation for Strategic Research (SSF)](https://strategiska.se/pressmeddelande/de-far-research-infrastructure-fellows/).

Through this five-year, SEK 15 million grant, we are developing and offering [smart microscopy](https://www.gu.se/en/core-facilities/centre-for-cellular-imaging/smart-microscopy) workflows to users of the [Centre for Cellular Imaging](https://www.gu.se/en/core-facilities/core-facilities-research-infrastructures/centre-for-cellular-imaging) at the [University of Gothenburg](https://www.gu.se/en).
:::
